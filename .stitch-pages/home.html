Arizona Insurance FAQs

Common questions about insuring homes in the desert environment.

Does standard home insurance cover monsoon wind and water damage?

expand_more
Wind damage (like roof shingles blowing off) is typically covered. However, flood damage caused by heavy monsoon rains pooling and entering your home from the ground up requires a separate Flood Insurance policy.

Are wildfires covered under my policy?

expand_more
Yes, fire is a standard covered peril. However, if you live in high-risk brush areas (like parts of Flagstaff, Prescott, or the foothills), getting coverage can be difficult. We work with specialized carriers who write policies in these specific zones.

Do I need extra liability for my swimming pool?

expand_more
Absolutely. A pool is considered an "attractive nuisance" and increases your liability risk significantly. We strongly recommend higher liability limits (at least $500,000) or an Umbrella policy for homes with pools.
