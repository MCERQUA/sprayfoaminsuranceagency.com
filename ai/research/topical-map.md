## 3. FAQ Page — 24 Questions (FAQPage schema-ready)

**URL:** `/faq` · **H1:** Arizona Homeowners Insurance FAQ

**General**
1. **How much does homeowners insurance cost in Arizona?** — AZ averages tend to run below the national average; premiums depend on dwelling value, roof age, location, and claims history. Expect a range and get a quote for exact pricing.
2. **Is homeowners insurance required in Arizona?** — Not by state law, but mortgage lenders require it until the loan is paid off.
3. **What does a standard homeowners policy cover?** — Dwelling, other structures, personal property, liability, loss of use, and medical payments to others.
4. **What is an HO-3 vs. HO-5 policy?** — HO-3 is open-peril on the structure and named-peril on contents; HO-5 extends open-peril to contents and is common for higher-value homes.
5. **How much dwelling coverage do I need?** — Enough to rebuild your home (replacement cost), not its market value or the land.
6. **Replacement cost vs. actual cash value — what's the difference?** — Replacement cost pays to rebuild/replace new; ACV subtracts depreciation, which matters a lot for AZ roofs.
7. **What's a home insurance deductible and how do I choose one?** — The amount you pay before coverage kicks in; higher deductibles lower premiums but raise out-of-pocket at claim time.
8. **Does home insurance cover my HVAC/AC unit?** — Yes if damaged by a covered peril (wind, hail, fire), but not for age-related breakdown unless you add equipment-breakdown coverage.
9. **How can I lower my Arizona home insurance premium?** — Bundle home + auto, raise your deductible, update roof/systems, add security/monitoring, and maintain good credit and a clean claims history.
10. **Does a new roof lower my premium?** — Often yes; newer roofs reduce claim risk and may qualify for RCV settlement instead of ACV.

**Arizona-specific**
11. **Does Arizona home insurance cover monsoon damage?** — Wind, hail, and rain entering through storm-caused openings are typically covered; flooding from the ground up is not.
12. **Is flood damage covered by homeowners insurance in AZ?** — No. Flooding (including flash floods and washes) requires a separate NFIP or private flood policy.
13. **Does home insurance cover haboob / dust-storm damage?** — Dust-storm wind damage to roofs, AC units, and structures is generally covered as wind; fine-dust cleanup alone may not be.
14. **Is wildfire damage covered by Arizona home insurance?** — Yes, fire (including wildfire) is a standard covered peril, but high-risk WUI homes may face non-renewal or need the AZ FAIR Plan.
15. **What is the Arizona FAIR Plan?** — A last-resort market for homeowners who can't get coverage in the standard market (often wildfire-exposed areas); it provides basic property coverage.
16. **Does insurance cover roof damage from sun and heat?** — No — UV/heat degradation is wear-and-tear and excluded; sudden storm/hail roof damage is covered.
17. **Does home insurance cover foundation damage from expansive soil?** — Earth-movement and settling are typically excluded; some water-related foundation damage may be covered depending on cause.
18. **Does my policy cover water/pipe damage?** — Sudden, accidental water discharge (burst pipe) is usually covered; long-term leaks, seepage, and neglect are not. Add water-backup coverage for drains/sump.
19. **Does home insurance cover my swimming pool?** — Pools are usually covered as "other structures," and pool liability makes adequate liability limits important in AZ.
20. **Why did my Arizona home insurance get non-renewed?** — Common reasons: wildfire/WUI exposure, roof age/condition, prior claims, or the carrier reducing risk in a ZIP code.
21. **Do I need extra coverage for a casita or guest house?** — It may fall under "other structures," but a casita rented out or used as an ADU can need additional coverage.
22. **Does home insurance cover monsoon tree damage to my house?** — Yes, damage from a fallen tree/limb during a storm is typically covered, including removal up to a limit.
23. **Should I insure my home for replacement cost given AZ rebuild prices?** — Yes; rising AZ construction costs mean extended or guaranteed replacement cost is worth considering to avoid underinsurance.
24. **How fast can I get a home insurance quote in Arizona?** — Usually same day — enter your address and details and we'll shop multiple carriers for you.
