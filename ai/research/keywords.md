### Data notes (read first)
- **Two location scopes were used.** The **Primary Keywords** volume table is pulled at **`Arizona, United States` (state-level)** — the geographically correct demand for this statewide site. The **Long-tail / suggestions** table is pulled at **`United States` (national)**, so its volumes run higher (national aggregate) — treat those as relative demand signals, not AZ-only counts.
- **KD (Keyword Difficulty)** is DataForSEO Labs 0–100. **CPC** is Google Ads USD. This niche has **extremely high CPC** ($40–$214) and **very low KD** (mostly 0–13) — a classic high-value, low-competition organic opportunity.
- `competition_level` came back null at state scope (Google Ads doesn't populate it for AZ-only geo); CPC is the reliable commercial-intent signal here.

## 6. Priority Targets (summary)

**Tier 1 — Money pages (build first):** high volume × low KD × high CPC.
- `home insurance arizona` / `arizona home insurance` (1,300 AZ / 2,400 US, KD 0, CPC $126–128) — **primary homepage/pillar target.**
- `cheap home insurance arizona` / `cheapest home insurance arizona` (170–260, KD 0, CPC $126–130) — dedicated "cheap/affordable" page.
- `home insurance quotes arizona` / `arizona home insurance quotes` (30–390, KD 5–9, CPC $96–171) — **the lead-gen quote page** (highest transactional intent).
- `homeowners insurance arizona` / `arizona homeowners insurance` (140, KD 0, CPC $86) — brand/pillar synonym cluster.

**Tier 2 — High-CPC city pages:** Scottsdale (KD 0, CPC $214), Flagstaff (CPC $174), Tucson (KD 0, CPC $115), Phoenix cluster (KD 2). Templated local landing pages, all 10 metros.

**Tier 3 — Product-line pages (low KD, real volume):** mobile/manufactured home insurance arizona (320–390, KD 2–4), renters insurance arizona (1,000, KD 13), condo insurance arizona (50, KD 0), landlord/dwelling insurance arizona (50, KD 20), home + auto bundle (KD 0–12, CPC $205).

**Tier 4 — Content/authority + FAQ (GEO & informational funnel):** "average cost of home insurance in arizona" + "why is arizona home insurance so high" + the AZ-peril angle set (monsoon, wildfire, roof/hail, heat/AC, foundation, water). Powers FAQ schema (use the 25 PAA questions in §3) and AI-search citations.

**Headline takeaway:** This niche is a rare combination of **very high commercial CPC ($40–$214/click)** and **very low organic difficulty (KD 0–13 on nearly every money term)**. An AZ-focused site can realistically rank the core money terms and city pages, and own the peril-content space that national carriers ignore.
